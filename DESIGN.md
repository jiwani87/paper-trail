--red:#b91c1c --green:#15803d --amber:#a16207 --amber-fill:#e8cf9c --link:#1d4ed8 --hi:#0b0a09`.
Two things had to change with it, and they are the trap for anyone re-theming this page again:
**the evidence quotes were a hardcoded `#d8dbe0`**, a pale grey with real contrast on near-black and
almost none on cream, which made the proof, the most important text on the screen, near-invisible;
they are `--quote:#332f2b` now, and no hardcoded colour is left in the stylesheet. Also
**a hover that went to `#fff` is invisible on cream**, so emphasis now goes DARKER via `--hi`; and
**amber filled solid was a highlight for a near-black ground** and on cream it became the loudest
thing on the screen, stealing the accent from the problem state, so the calendar's standby cells use
`--amber-fill` while amber TEXT keeps full strength where it is the one number that earned it.
The dark page is kept at `generated/issues-dark-backup.html`.

## The screen

1. **One-word heading.** "Issues". No eyebrow line, no subtitle, no report count above or below.
   The three numbers (issues, notices not served, standby days) sit top right, computed from the
   trace, a small label under each.
2. **Hairlines, not boxes.** A 1 px line above each item on the dark ground. No card fill, border,
   radius or shadow anywhere.
3. **One accent colour, for the problem state.** Red for a missed window and NOT SERVED. Grey for
   handled. Green only on the SERVED tag and its date. Amber only on the standby-days figure.
4. **Status is text, and one state per element.** Uppercase mono, letter-spaced, coloured. No chip,
   no pill, no border. A tag says one thing: SERVED or NOT SERVED. "NOT SERVED · CONTESTED" was two
   states in one tag and came off on 12 Sep (MJ: "again you are writing too many status in one go");
   contested keeps its own cell in the strip on screen 2, where it has a date.
5. **Nothing repeats.** The strip on screen 2 runs in date order and is the notice path. There is
   no second row of the same dates. Before adding an element, name what it shows that nothing else
   on that screen shows.
6a. **Nine issues means FIVE rows in a column, not four, and the card has to fit the row it gets.**
   MJ, 18 Sep: "push everything little up last issue seems rather down". With `grid-auto-rows:1fr` a
   five-row column gives each card ~175px, and a card carrying a standby line measured ~196px, so the
   fifth one sat on the bottom edge. Fixed by trimming the card, not the grid: `.card` padding
   `22px 0 22px` and gap 14 (was `30px 0 34px`, gap 18), and the screen's top padding 56 to 40 with the
   head gap 24 to 18 to lift the whole block. **Measured after any change to this: the last card must
   clear the bottom of the 1920x1080 frame by ~48px on the 9-issue page. check_fit does NOT see this**,
   because nothing clips; it is the last card sitting against the edge, which only a measurement finds.

6. **Fill the frame.** Nothing scrolls, no dead half, no dead band at the bottom. The register's
   grid takes the whole height under the heading and divides it into equal rows (`grid-auto-rows:1fr`),
   so a card without a standby line is as tall as its neighbour; screen 2 centres its block
   (rule 18) and flows the evidence into columns (rule 16).
7. **Cards earn their place with the bar.** First seen to cleared, the missed notice window in red,
   all grey when served. Take the bar away and this must become a table again.
   **Every dated event once, as a label and a date** (MJ 12 Sep 13:33, "more clean"): the tag is
   SERVED with its date, or NOT SERVED; the middle slot is "Due 16 Mar", red when the window was
   missed, grey when served. The words "missed" and "served 16 Mar" after the due date, and the
   letter ref in the tag, all came off: the tag and the bar already said missed, and the ref is on
   the correspondence tab. Under the dates, "Standby N days" when the trace records it, and nothing
   else: party and clause came off the card too (MJ 12 Sep 13:40, "unnecessary").
8. **Labels in Title Case.** Set in `SHORT` at the top of the generator, one place.
9. **Nothing typed that the trace does not record.** Header numbers are summed from the status
   column. The consequence line is "Standby N days" when the trace says so, otherwise nothing.
10. **The letter is a letter.** `letters/<REF>.md`, one per cited ref that has been written,
    rendered to PDF by `tools/letter2pdf.py` (rule 17). Letterhead, ref, date, addressee, subject,
    salutation, paragraphs, signature, sign-off. Third person, every clause from
    `samples/CONTRACT-extract.md`. No "sample" wording, no caption.
11. **No notes, footnotes, captions or helper text.** Global rule, applies here in full.
12. **Type.** IBM Plex Sans for words, IBM Plex Mono for dates, refs and clause numbers. Google
    Fonts with system fallbacks, so the page still reads with no network.

## The frame

The stage is always 1920x1080 and scales to the window (`--s`). Centre it by absolute position,
not grid. Measure fit with the stage's `getBoundingClientRect()`, never `scrollWidth` (body is
`overflow:hidden`, so an overflow reads as a clean fit). `tools/check_fit.js` is the gate.

13. **Screen 2 opens on the context, never on the evidence.** Title, the model's own sentence for
    the issue under it, the strip, the calendar, then one row of three words and one panel.
14. **The row is CONTEXT · EVIDENCE · CORRESPONDENCE.** Uppercase mono, hairline above, ink when on,
    link blue when not. Text only: no button, no underline, no chevron, no count after Evidence. The
    row sits under the calendar, where the panel it controls is, not on the bottom edge of the frame.
15. **Context is the model's prose, written to five beats, and scored.** Four to six sentences from
    the trace's CONTEXT table (skill Table 3), one per line, 19 px, ink, each line with a hollow 8 px
    grey circle before it (MJ 12 Sep 15:30: "bullet point, show the circle icon"; same on NEXT). MJ
    12 Sep 15:00 on the first
    version: "disconnected sentence... at least some dates should be mentioned and sentence should
    have some context, it feels empty." The framework now in SKILL.md, one beat per sentence: the
    event dated and placed against what should have happened; what the site did; what came back;
    how it ended with the standby days; the paper position with the date the window closed. Every
    sentence anchored in time (a date, or "the same day" / "by then" off the sentence before, never
    both). No report numbers, letter refs, clause or section numbers. `tools/eval_context.py` scores
    it: hard defects (invented dates, unsupported figures, references, bar 0) and the framework
    checks (thresholds proposed, MJ to set). It cannot see whether the story is the right story:
    the 12 Sep third pass said "none served" on HDD-05 where the query had met the ground window,
    caught by eye, now a check. Then MJ's reading of 16:10: on HDD-05 the ground notice under PC 7.2
    is the whole requirement (PC 12.2), so context never calls a time-and-cost window missed for it;
    skill date rule 7, and a check. Assembling context from trace rows with a ref on every line was
    tried and read as robotic; so did undated sentences.
16. **Evidence is every line, three columns past nine, each opening its PDF.** The line above each
    quote is report and date only; the section number the checker uses stays in the data and off the
    screen (MJ 12 Sep 06:20: "this number has no meaning, remove"). No rule down the
    left and no indent: the three panels share one left edge with the tab labels and one body size,
    19 px, with the 15 px mono line above each item. (MJ 12 Sep 06:00: the old column rule read as
    a stray line, and context ran bigger than the lists.)
17. **Correspondence is the thread.** Every letter, email and delivery note the trace cites for the
    issue, in date order: ref · date · to or from Maradel, then the subject. An item opens
    `letters/<REF>.pdf` when the file exists; without one it is listed in grey and does not respond.
    Four of seventeen have a document, on purpose. The PDF is rendered by `tools/letter2pdf.py`
    (12 Sep 06:10, "make it look more professional"): letterhead with a rule and the subcontract
    number, a Ref · Date · To block, bold subject, body at 10.5 pt, a drawn signature (one stroke
    set per company, dark ink, in the renderer), the signatory's title, footer with company and ref.
    One A4 page, no address invented. Subjects are one line, under about 65 characters (MJ 06:15:
    "the subject is too long, and no signature"); chainages and clause detail stay in the body.
18. **On every tab the block sits just above centre** (spacers 0.85 : 1). When the panel is tall the
    spacers have nothing to share and the block starts at the top, no slide. Content fades in on each
    tab. Keys for recording: C, E, R, Esc.
19. **Every calendar cell opens its day's report.** Every day has one. The thin outline stays on
    the days the model cited, so the strip still shows where the evidence came from (MJ 12 Sep
    06:20: "every cell should link with a report").
20. **Every cell carries its date**, in grey; the key days keep their colour (first seen and cleared
    in ink, due in red, served in green). The standby key appears only on an issue that has standby
    days; a key with nothing on the strip to match it invited the question "how did you count
    7 days of standby" on an issue with none (MJ 12 Sep 06:21).
21. **The strip**: clock started, notice due, served, reply or contested or cleared, then IMPACT.
    No NEXT cell (MJ 12 Sep 15:30: "dont show next in status line... you are bloating stuff"). The Responsible cell was removed (MJ 12 Sep 06:35:
    "too detail"), and party · clause left the register card at 13:40, so the party is named only in
    the context prose and the correspondence, and the clause only where a date carries it (Notice
    due, Next). Values 18 px mono, one line,
    never wrapping; `check_fit.js` fails a cell whose value is wider than the cell.
22. **Nothing above the calendar but the key.** The "n days from first seen" span and the standby
    line were both removed (MJ 06:30 and 06:45: "confusing", "extra info"). IMPACT carries the
    standby days, amber when there are any, "0 days" in grey when there are none. "For now we write
    impact no. of days" (MJ): slower or extra work has no day count in the trace yet.
23. **NEXT is the contract's next step, in the model's words.** Skill Table 4: a date, a clause and
    one to three sentences (do this by then; or this was lost and this can still be done; or closed).
    On the NEXT tab only, same voice and size as Context, never in the strip. Unlike Context, every
    NEXT sentence names the clause it rests on (MJ 12 Sep 16:10: "every time you treat something in
    next clearly write under which clause"). It must agree with Table 1: a notice recorded served is
    named, not denied. Ground notified under PC 7.2 is a Variation under PC 12.2 and that notice is
    the whole requirement; NEXT never calls a Clause 9 notice missed for it or says time or money
    was lost (MJ 12 Sep: "HDD-05 there was no need for notice for time and cost"; the second bullet
    that said so was removed by re-running the call under the corrected rule, not by hand). It is advice; no gate checks it, and
    on camera it is the model's proposal for MJ to adjudicate. Written for I-04 only on 12 Sep
    ("build first for 1 event and show me").

`tools/check_fit.js` opens every issue on every tab at 1920x1080 and fails if a panel needs more
